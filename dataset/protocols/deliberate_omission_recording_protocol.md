# D Y Patil Medical College: Foley Catheterization OSCE Simulation Recording Protocol
## Targeted Deliberate-Omission Cohort Expansion (Phase 6 Protocol)

**Objective:** Capture 25 to 30 structured, multi-angle OSCE assessment videos to establish statistically balanced negative ground-truth cohorts for all 13 catheterization steps, enabling certified automated evaluation.

---

### 1. Equipment & Camera Setup
- **Primary Work-Area Camera (Cam 1):** 1920x1080 @ 60 fps, fixed tripod mounted at 45° angle facing the mannequin pelvic area and instrument trolley (distance ~1.8 m, height ~1.3 m).
- **Secondary Overhead Camera (Cam 2, Optional):** Mounted directly above the mannequin bed facing straight down onto the sterile field.
- **Audio Capture:** Directional lapel microphone or boundary room mic placed within 1.5 m of the student and mannequin to capture candidate verbal explanation and examiner verbal prompts clearly without clipping.
- **Lighting:** Consistent overhead surgical simulation lighting (~500–700 lux). Avoid direct glare on sterile packaging.

---

### 2. The 13 Standard Procedural Steps & Mark Start Criteria

| Step # | Clinical Step Name | Official Mark Start Moment |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Introduce and explain procedure | Student first addresses the patient / speaks introduction |
| **2** | Informed consent | Student explicitly asks patient for permission or consent |
| **3** | Check instruments & expiry | Student first handles or inspects items on the sterile tray |
| **4** | Hand hygiene and sterile gloves | Hand rub begins or first sterile glove wrapper is opened |
| **5** | Patient privacy, position, exposure | Screen moved or patient gown/sheet lifted to expose genitalia |
| **6** | Painting and sterile draping | First antiseptic swab touches patient, followed by fenestrated drape |
| **7** | Lignocaine jelly instillation | Tip of lignocaine jelly syringe/tube touches urethral meatus |
| **8** | Open catheter and insert | Catheter lifted from packaging and tip inserted into meatus |
| **9** | Attach urobag, confirm urine | Catheter connected to drainage bag tubing and urine return noted |
| **10** | Inflate retention balloon | Water syringe attached to balloon port and plunger depressed |
| **11** | Traction until resistance | Catheter gently pulled back until balloon snugs at bladder neck |
| **12** | Reposition prepuce | Foreskin pulled forward over glans penis to prevent paraphimosis |
| **13** | Catheter tip dressing / securing | Dressing applied to meatus or catheter secured to thigh |

### 3. Targeted Deliberate-Omission Script Matrix (25 Sessions)

| Script Group | Sessions | Prescribed Deliberate Action / Omission | Intended Grade | Target Negative Steps |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Group A: Glove Omission** | 5 sessions | Student touches sterile items directly with bare unwashed hands, omitting Step 4 | **R** | Step 4 (Hygiene & Gloves) |
| **Group B: Drape Omission** | 5 sessions | Student paints meatus but completely omits placing fenestrated drape (Step 6) | **R** | Step 6 (Painting & Draping) |
| **Group C: Lignocaine Omission** | 3 sessions | Student attempts catheter insertion without anaesthetic lubrication (omits Step 7) | **R** | Step 7 (Lignocaine Jelly) |
| **Group D: Balloon Omission** | 4 sessions | Student inserts catheter and attaches bag, but forgets to inflate balloon (Step 10) | **R** | Step 10 (Inflate Balloon) |
| **Group E: Traction Omission** | 3 sessions | Balloon inflated, but student does not pull back catheter to bladder neck (Step 11) | **R** | Step 11 (Traction until resistance) |
| **Group F: Paraphimosis Risk** | 3 sessions | Procedure completed, but foreskin is left retracted (omits Step 12) | **R** | Step 12 (Reposition Prepuce) |
| **Group G: Examiner Verbal Prompts**| 5 sessions | Student hesitates at Step 4, 6, 8, 10, or 12 until examiner verbally cues them | **P-1** | Prompt detection test |

---

### 4. Faculty Adjudication & Recording Log Sheet

For each recorded session, the attending faculty member must complete:
- **Video ID:** `SIM_REC_XXX`
- **Candidate ID:** `Resident_Name / ID`
- **Prescribed Script:** Group A–G or Standard Unscripted Pass
- **Actual Performed Sequence:** Timestamps for each step
- **Prompt Log:** Timestamp and exact words spoken by examiner if any
- **Final Faculty Grade:** P, P-1, or R with signature
